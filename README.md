# **Digital Marketing Portfolio**





### **Project Overview**



This portfolio showcases digital marketing analytics work completed during my previous professional experience, using three months of historical marketing data.



The project includes Google Ads campaign performance analysis, Google Analytics 4 (GA4) website analytics, Power BI dashboards, Excel-based data analysis, and examples of social media content created for multiple platforms.



The objective was to evaluate marketing performance, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and derive actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.





### **Project Objectives**



\- Analyze Google Ads campaign performance and advertising efficiency.

\- Evaluate website traffic, user engagement, and conversion metrics using Google Analytics 4 (GA4).

\- Analyze social media performance across multiple platforms.

\- Prepare and analyze marketing data using Microsoft Excel.

\- Develop Power BI dashboards to visualize marketing KPIs and performance trends.

\- Identify optimization opportunities through data analysis.

\- Showcase social media content created for different platforms.





### **Tools and Technologies**



\- Power BI: Dashboard development, KPI tracking, data visualization, and performance reporting.

\- Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Website traffic, user engagement, traffic acquisition, and conversion analysis.

\- Google Ads: Paid advertising performance and campaign analysis.

\- Microsoft Excel: Data preparation, cleaning, analysis, and reporting.

\- Social Media Platforms: Content creation and review of post-level performance.
